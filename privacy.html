What we collect

When you send a booking request we receive your name, your chosen stay, dates and number of guests. If you send it on WhatsApp or by email, we also see the number or email address you message us from.

How we use it

  • Only to reply to your request, confirm your stay and look after you during it.
  • Your request reaches us on WhatsApp or by email. WhatsApp requests also send us a copy by email through the FormSubmit email service.
  • We never sell your details or use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep booking messages only as long as needed for your stay and for our accounting records.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details. Just email us.

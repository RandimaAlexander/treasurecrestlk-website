The Villa

A Different
Kind of Stay

A private sanctuary shaped by tropical mornings, warm ocean breezes and the timeless heritage of Galle. Treasure Crest is an exclusive 3-bedroom villa created for guests seeking total privacy, quiet elegance and effortless Sri Lankan hospitality. Not a crowded hotel. Not a commercial resort. Your own private world between the palms and the sea.

Built in 1895 for a wealthy cinnamon merchant, the house was restored with care: thick old walls, carved timber and colonial windows, with beds dressed in local fabrics, soap made with Ceylon cinnamon, and jungle views from every room.
